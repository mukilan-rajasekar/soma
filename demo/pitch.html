The problem

Brands spend billions on video ads and test them with guesses.

Ad pre-testing is slow, expensive, and ungrounded. Panels and surveys take days to weeks and cost thousands per study — and they capture what people say, not how a brain is likely to respond. Meanwhile a performance team ships dozens of paid-social videos a week; they can't wait for a panel, so they post and pray. Incumbents (Nielsen, System1, Realeyes) are built for a handful of hero spots. The high-volume, low-cost end — where most video actually gets made — is unserved.

test 1, ship 30 blind.
The insight / wedge

The brain's response to video is now a public model.

Meta released TRIBE — winner of Algonauts 2025 — which predicts second-by-second brain (fMRI) activation from video, benchmarked against real human scans. We don't reinvent the brain; we build the honest last mile on public science: upload an ad, get a predicted cortical response in minutes, from the file — no panel, no webcam, no survey. Our edge isn't the model anyone can download. It's a hard, visible line between what's validated (video→brain) and what's hypothesis (brain→affect). Competitors sell opaque, un-auditable accuracy you take on faith; we sell a transparent, reproducible signal. Honesty is the wedge they structurally can't copy.

ad video→PUBLIC TRIBE (Algonauts-2025 winner)→predicted cortical response→interest + affect arcs
The product

A predicted, group-average read of your ad — second by second.

Upload a video. Soma runs TRIBE and reads off a predicted interest/attention arc (where the brain engages vs tunes out) now, and an experimental, not-yet-validated affect read next (valence = feels good/bad, arousal = calm/excited). It flags weak spots in plain language: "the model predicts attention drops at 0:07 — two seconds before your logo lands." Repeatable, per-file, in minutes. A pre-flight check before spend, not a research project after it.

Live demo (index.html): the ad plays with the predicted arcs scrubbing in sync + a weak-spot flag pinned to the timeline. Real screen recording, not a mockup.
Validation — we show our work

Exactly where the line is — including what isn't proven.

  • validatedVideo → brain activation. Public TRIBE, benchmarked vs real fMRI. Public weights — that's the point, we didn't re-prove the hard science.
  • running this weekInterest/attention arc vs TVSum. Pre-registered, within-video, circular-shift permutation null, n = time. Result pending — may return null. Slot: p = ____, n = ____.
  • hypothesisActivation → affect. Cortex-only, group-average, not yet validated. Being tested vs public LIRIS-ACCEDE per-second ratings.
  • not claimedNamed emotions, retention lift. Earned rungs, not marketing.

Honest downside: cortex-only + group-average — we do NOT measure a real individual (Realeyes does). The signal source and the honesty are the edge, not the arc's shape.

The moat is not the public model. It's (1) the honest inference layer, (2) a proprietary data flywheel — ads × real reactions × sales outcomes — that compounds with every customer, and (3) the daily workflow. Why won't Realeyes build it? A public, transparent model directly undercuts their "trust our panel" pitch and per-study pricing — a business-model conflict, not an engineering gap.

Market + who pays

DTC & performance teams drowning in paid-social video.

Beachhead: DTC brands and growth teams running high-volume paid social — the low end Nielsen/System1/Realeyes ignore because their panel model is too slow and pricey per asset. They test dozens of creatives a week with real budget tied to spend, so a per-video pre-flight check pays for itself. Who pays: growth and creative leads, on per-seat / per-video SaaS priced far below a panel study. The value anchor: flagging where the model predicts an attention drop (signal under validation) before you commit six figures of media. Better than prompting GPT?
