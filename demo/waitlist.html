Pre-test your video ads against the predicted brain response — before you spend.

We predict the average viewer's second-by-second cortical response to a video ad with Meta's public TRIBE v2, then read an attention arc (and a coarse affect arc) off it — from the file, no human panel.

The honest one-liner: everyone doing synthetic ad testing prompts an LLM to role-play a consumer. We predict the actual neural response using a brain-encoding model trained on real fMRI — the grounding the category lacks.

what we will and won't claim

The attention arc is pre-registered and being validated right now — it may return null, and if it does we'll say so. Affect (valence / arousal) is an unvalidated hypothesis. Everything is a group average, cortex only — not one person's feeling, and never a confident single named-emotion score. We ship a claim only after a held-out test reproduces it.
