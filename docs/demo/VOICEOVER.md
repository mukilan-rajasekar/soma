# /demo — voiceover script (~60s)

Timed for a single top-to-bottom scroll of `usesoma.work/demo`. Each beat is pinned to a
section; the target time is when you should *finish* that beat. Every section animates on
entry, so land on it, let the animation play (~1s), then move. Total ~60s. Copy is written
to be read at a calm, confident pace — trim the bracketed asides if you run long.

Delivery note: don't read the on-screen copy back. The voiceover is the layer *over* it.

---

**[0:00 — Hero · "Anyone can make a hundred ads"]**  (~7s)
> "Anyone can generate a hundred ads in minutes. The hard part isn't making them — it's
> knowing which one will actually work. That's what Soma does."

**[0:08 — §1 The science]**  (~10s)
> "It reads the brain directly. And attention isn't one thing — two different regions do
> two different jobs. The dorsal network up here is where focus is steered. The ventral
> network, the salience system, fires on surprise — on anything the brain didn't see
> coming. We measure them separately. Eye-tracking can't do that."

**[0:18 — §2 Hook scoring]**  (~9s)
> "The first three seconds are the hook — scored on their own, and worth almost half the
> total. And it's that ventral surprise signal that measures it: a hook works by being
> unexpected. Here the surprise response spikes at two seconds. The opening lands."

**[0:27 — §3 Comprehension]**  (~8s)
> "Then — did the product actually register? Soma reads the ad's own words, on screen and
> out loud, and when the brand is named and the language cortex confirms it, the score
> rises."

**[0:35 — §4 Ranked batch]**  (~8s)
> "Give it a batch — ten ads in, one ranking out. And every score breaks into its drivers,
> so you don't just see which ad won, you see why, and what to fix on the ones that didn't."

**[0:43 — §5 Same-campaign cuts]**  (~6s)
> "Take one campaign, five different cuts, on one timeline. They split apart in the opening
> seconds — where the edit decides everything."

**[0:49 — §6 Dip + shot diagnosis]**  (~7s)
> "Soma finds exactly where attention drops — and goes shot by shot. Cut this opening shot,
> the score climbs. That one shot was dragging the whole ad down. Diagnosis at the shot,
> not a single grade."

**[0:56 — §7 Watch it live → §8 moat]**  (~5s)
> "Watch any ad next to its score, live. And every batch a customer runs makes the model
> sharper. Soma finds the winners."

*(End on the CTA. If you have a couple of seconds spare, hold on the live player and press
play so the ad moves under the arc.)*

---

## Tighter 45s cut (if you need to trim)

Drop §5 and the §8 moat line; compress §1 to "Attention isn't one thing — dorsal steers
focus, ventral fires on surprise, measured separately." Keep hero → science → hook →
comprehension → ranking → dip → live. That's the spine.

## The one line that matters most

If a partner only remembers one sentence, make it the hero + §4:
> "Making ads is solved. Knowing which one wins isn't — Soma reads how a brain watches each
> cut and finds the winner."
