## 3. Marin Software (cautionary tale — company effectively gone)

- **Status**: Board announced Plan of Dissolution Apr 10, 2025 (https://investor.marinsoftware.com/Investor-News/Investor-News-Details/2025/Marin-Software-Announces-Plan-of-Dissolution/default.aspx); delisted from Nasdaq Jun 26, 2025; **Chapter 11 filed Jul 1, 2025**; assets sold via pre-negotiated plan to Kaxxa Holdings (ESW Capital affiliate), effective Sep 5, 2025 (https://elevenflo.com/blog/marin-software-bankruptcy-pre-negotiated-chapter-11, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marin_Software).
- Current site is a placeholder: "Marin Software has been acquired by Zax Capital... entering its AI-first era" promising Predict / Automate / Prove; no live product pages. (https://www.marinsoftware.com) **Any current product capability is UNVERIFIED.**
- **Historic capabilities (MarinOne, for the record)**: unified enterprise management of paid search (Google/Microsoft), paid social, and ecommerce/retail ads; bid/budget optimization; in Apr 2024 launched an "AI-powered Anomaly Detector" for performance campaigns. (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marin_Software)
- **Why it died** (relevant to Soma): commoditization of ad-management tools and free/low-cost native tooling from Google/Meta eroded the value of a thin management layer; ~$350M cumulative losses, never a profitable year; much of terminal revenue was Google rev-share, not product. (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marin_Software, https://elevenflo.com/blog/marin-software-bankruptcy-pre-negotiated-chapter-11) Lesson: a cross-channel dashboard + rules engine alone is not defensible; creative production + proprietary optimization data are what Smartly/Skai survived on.

## Table stakes

Capabilities that appear across MOST of these platforms — what the enterprise market treats as mandatory for a "full-service" creative+media ad platform:

1. **Feed/catalog ingestion + enrichment** — connect any product feed (Shopify, Sheets, retailer feeds), clean/enrich it, keep it auto-synced, detect errors. (Smartly Unified Catalog Mgmt; Hunch Catalogs; Skai Retail Ops; StackAdapt CRM/data ingestion)
2. **Template-based creative scaling** — design once (with Figma/Photoshop import), bind layers to data, render thousands of on-brand variants auto-adapted to every format/placement. (Smartly, Hunch; Skai Creative Center adjacently)
3. **DCO / dynamic personalization** — creative assembled per audience from feed + context signals (geo, language, weather, time of day, price/promo). (Smartly, Hunch, StackAdapt dynamic creative, Skai Dynamic Commerce Ads)
4. **Video automation** — image-to-video, catalog product video, template video at scale. (Smartly, Hunch; the current battleground — both lead with it)
5. **Localization at scale** — language/geo/store-level variants generated automatically. (Smartly, Hunch)
6. **Creative collaboration + approval workflow** — a "mission control" where teams adapt, review/approve, and launch. (Smartly Producer; Hunch creative review; Skai Creative Center briefing/reviews)
7. **Multi-account, multi-platform campaign management from one UI** — bulk creation, bulk edit, campaign mirroring, unified UX across channels. (all four)
8. **Automation rules/triggers** — pause/scale/alert on conditions; plan-driven or rule-driven always-on campaign maintenance. (Smartly Optimization Triggers; Hunch Autopilot + smart rules; Skai Advanced Automated Actions; Marin historically)
9. **AI/predictive budget allocation + pacing** — budgets shifted across campaigns/channels toward predicted winners, with forecasting and pacing guardrails. (Smartly PBA; Skai portfolio optimization + budget pacing/forecasting; StackAdapt real-time forecasting)
10. **Custom optimization goals from 1st/3rd-party data** — optimize to GA4/Adobe, MMP (AppsFlyer/Adjust), CRM/LTV, offline data — not just platform-reported conversions. (Smartly, Hunch, Skai, StackAdapt)
11. **Cross-channel reporting dashboard** — custom metrics, custom views, scheduled/shareable reports (email/Slack/Teams). (all)
12. **Creative-level insights/tagging** — auto-classify creative attributes and attribute performance to them ("why it works"). (Smartly Creative Insights; Skai Creative AI; Hunch product/creative insights)
13. **Experimentation + incrementality** — lift studies, incrementality testing, and increasingly incrementality-as-an-optimization-signal. (Skai Impact Navigator; Smartly + INCRMNTAL; Hunch via Meta lift studies; StackAdapt Brand Lift)
14. **Rapid support for native platform formats** — Advantage+ Shopping, PMax, Demand Gen, Catalog Product Video within weeks of platform release; positioning as Meta/TikTok Business Partner. (Smartly, Hunch, Skai)
15. **Enterprise wrapper: services + trust** — dedicated CSM, managed onboarding, optional full managed service, 24/7 support, academy/certification, security compliance (SOC 2 / ISO 27001), multi-brand/agency account structures, API access. (Smartly, Skai strongly; Hunch via bundled experts; SLAs publicly documented by no one — **UNVERIFIED** across the board)

**2025–2026 AI pattern**: every survivor ships (a) generative creative production (Smartly AI Studio), (b) predictive pre-flight creative scoring (Smartly CPP attention/eye-tracking models — directly overlapping Soma's neural ad scoring), (c) predictive budget allocation, and (d) a GenAI agent/copilot over campaign data (Skai Celeste, StackAdapt Ivy Studio). Marin — a rules-and-reporting layer without creative production or proprietary optimization signal — is the one that died.
