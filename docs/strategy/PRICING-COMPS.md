# Pricing comps — agencies and automated ad platforms

Fetched **2026-08-07**. Living appendix for fee *structures*, not for neural-tester
positioning (`COMPETITORS.md` stays that SoT and should link here).

Honesty rule: a number without a URL + fetch date is `[NEEDS EVIDENCE]` and is not
deck-eligible. Third-party blogs are labelled. Vendor marketing pages beat blogs.
Smartly does **not** publish a price page; treat every Smartly % as second-hand.

Soma’s own model (for comparison only): **one weekly all-in price = media + 20% margin,
renew until paused.** Media is prepaid-week capped; margin is not spendable. Quotes ≠
invoices (`BUILD-PLAN-REVENUE.md`).

multi-thousand monthly retainer, paid **on top of** media you still fund at the
platforms.” Soma’s 20% sits **inside** one weekly bill.

## Implications for Soma’s pitch (not new decisions)

1. **Structure first, rate second** — still true. Lead with one weekly all-in number.
2. **20% is agency-band, not Smartly-band.** That is honest if we are selling a managed
   service (creative + placement + optimization), dishonest if we are selling a dashboard.
3. **Weekly vs monthly** is packaging; $1.5k/week ≈ $6k/mo retainer-shaped. Say weekly
   because that is what the product renews on.
4. **Do not invoice** until gate 4 + counsel. Comps do not unblock charging.
